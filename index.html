Christian Holy Matrimony

Christian Weddings

Celebrated in a church with scripture readings, exchange of rings, and vows led by a priest or pastor. A timeless journey of unconditional love, grace, and lifelong companionship.

Cathedral Church Vows: Grand walk down the flower-lined aisle with live choir music, surrounded by family warmth and blessings.
Scripture Readings of Love: Sacred gospel verses celebrating patient kindness, mutual care, and divine lifelong devotion.
Exchange of Rings & Vows: Solemn exchange of blessed wedding bands and vows before the altar, sealing an eternal bond.
Priestly Nuptial Blessing: Holy prayer of togetherness followed by joyous bell chimes, cake cutting, and celebratory reception feast.

Hindu Vedic Nuptials

Hindu Weddings

Multi-day events featuring rituals like the Mandap (sacred canopy), Haldi (turmeric paste application), and Saat Phere (seven vows around the holy fire).

Mandap (Sacred Canopy): Grand floral sanctum with royal pillars and sacred havan fire embodying cosmic harmony.
Haldi & Pithi Ritual: Purifying organic turmeric paste application with affectionate family blessings for glowing love.
Saat Phere & Saptapadi: Seven sacred vows taken around the holy fire uniting two souls in lifelong devotion and mutual care.
Kanyadaan & Varmala: Emotional exchange of fragrant rose garlands and parental blessings welcoming a new life chapter.
Ceremony Duration: 3 to 5 Days Celebrations Plan Hindu Wedding
Muslim Nikah & Walima

Muslim Weddings (Nikah)

Involves the formal signing of a marriage contract (Nikahnama) and community prayers, followed by a feast. A sacred union built on trust, respect, and deep affection.

Nikahnama Signing: Formal legal and spiritual marriage contract signed before witnesses with divine grace.
Ijab-e-Qubool: Sacred declaration of mutual consent ("Qubool Hai") solemnized by the Qazi in an atmosphere of pure emotion.
Dua & Quranic Blessings: Heartfelt prayers offered for lifelong peace, harmony, family prosperity, and love.
Grand Walima Banquet: Opulent celebratory royal reception feast hosted with warmth to share happiness with all guests.
Ceremony Duration: Nikah + Grand Walima Feast Plan Muslim Nikah
Sikh Anand Karaj

Sikh Weddings (Anand Karaj)

Held in a Gurdwara, centering on four circumambulations (lavan) of the holy scripture, the Guru Granth Sahib. A blissful union where two bodies share one single light.

Gurdwara Sanctum & Kirtan: Peaceful congregational seating with divine floral darbar decor and soulful hymns.
Four Sacred Lavan: Four circumambulations around Sri Guru Granth Sahib signifying spiritual journey and steadfast devotion.
Palla Rasam: Father hands the groom’s palla to the bride, signifying mutual support, trust, and eternal protection.
Ardas & Karah Parshad: Solemn collective prayer of gratitude followed by the sweet distribution of blessed prashad.
Ceremony Duration: Morning Anand Karaj + Grand Langar Plan Sikh Wedding
The Complete Grand Journey

Typical 7-Day Indian Wedding Timeline

Experience the step-by-step grandeur of authentic Indian nuptials. From intimate home blessings to electrifying sangeet beats and grand reception galas.

Day 1

Day 1: Roka & Sagaai (Engagement)

Formal announcement of the union.

Exchanging of rings and gifts between families.
Formal agreement and sweet exchange (mithai distribution) uniting both clans.
Official Union Announcement
Day 2

Day 2: Ganesh Puja & Vighna Harta

Prayers offered to Lord Ganesha at respective family homes to remove obstacles.

Intimate home-based blessings with Vedic mantras.
Invoking peace, prosperity, and obstacle-free wedding festivities.
Spiritual Blessings
Day 3

Day 3: Mehendi (Henna Ceremony)

Application of intricate henna designs on the bride's hands and feet.

Music, folk songs, and celebratory food with close female relatives and friends.
Artisan bridal henna, floral jewelry favors, and colorful bohemian lounge decor.
Henna & Folk Celebration
Day 4

Day 4: Sangeet & Cocktail Night

Grand musical celebration where both families perform choreographed dances.

Feasting, socializing, and welcoming extended guests.
High-tech LED stage, celebrity DJ sets, cocktail lounge, and cold pyro FX.
Dance & Musical Gala
Day 5

Day 5: Haldi & Pithi Ceremony

Application of turmeric, sandalwood, and oil paste on the bride and groom for glowing, purified skin.

Playful and emotional family ritual held separately at each home.
Marigold floral showers, traditional dholak beats, and yellow ethnic dress codes.
Glow & Purification Ritual
Day 6 (Main Day)

Day 6: The Wedding Day (Vivah)

The sacred matrimonial climax uniting two souls and families under the divine Mandap.

Baraat: Groom's energetic grand procession with music and dancing.
Varmala: Exchanging of floral garlands.
Saat Phere / Saptapadi: The sacred seven steps and vows taken around the holy fire under the mandap.
Bidaai: Emotional farewell as the bride leaves her parental home.
The Sacred Vivah
Day 7

Day 7: Reception (Grand Finale) & Grihapravesh

The grand celebratory culmination introducing the newly married couple to society.

Grihapravesh: Formal welcoming of the bride into her new marital home.
Reception: Large social banquet hosted by the groom's family to introduce the newlyweds to society.
Grand Finale Banquet
